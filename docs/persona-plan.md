# KERI Personas & Persuasion Plan

KERI's and SEDI's challenge — captured in the value system itself as the *missing* values
**MARKETPEN** (market penetration), **GRASPEASY** (easy to grasp), **MATURITY** and
**COMMUNEDU** (community education) — is not a technology gap. It is a persuasion gap:
the people who could adopt KERI never hear an argument framed in *their* values.

This plan defines six personas, maps each one to the KERI value tags that matter to them,
and gives a concrete plan to convince each. Every persona gets a **clip reel** from the
KERICONF26 talks, played with the video gallery’s clip player (see `docs/persona-clips.md`
and `src/data/keri-personas.json`, generated by `npm run build-personas`), because
*seeing is believing* — which is literally part of the MARKETPEN node description.

## The core insight

Do not sell "KERI". Sell the outcome each persona already wants, give less priority to the MINIMSUFFI value. SEDI is much more the means, SECUFIRST is first and foremost value, KERI is a toolset to adapt the technical means to the plan. Each persona below therefore has:

- **Who they are** and what keeps them up at night
- **Value tags** — the nodes in the graph that speak to them (their clip filter)
- **Key message** — one sentence they should repeat to their peers
- **Objections & counters** — what they will say, and the honest answer
- **Tactics** — where and how to reach them
- **Call to action** — the smallest next step we ask of them

## Persona 1 — "Sarah", the Enterprise CISO / Security Architect

**Who:** Accountable for breaches at a bank, insurer, or critical-infrastructure firm.
Lives in a world of phished credentials, deepfakes, and supply-chain compromise. Buys
outcomes, not protocols.

**Value tags:** SECUFIRST, FAULTTOL, OTHUNSAFE, ANTIFRAUD, VERNOTVAL, NOSHARSECR, ENTROPY

**Key message:** *"KERI assumes you will be breached and lets you recover — pre-rotated
keys mean stolen credentials are dead on arrival, and everything is verifiable, not
merely validated."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"Nobody else uses it."* → Neither did TLS in 1996. Show GLEIF vLEI in production;
  regulated finance is already the beachhead.
- *"My stack is X.509/JWT."* → KERI surfs existing infrastructure (INFRASURF): OOBIs via
  X.509, VVP on JWT. It's an overlay, not a rip-and-replace.
- *"Too immature."* → Concede MATURITY honestly; offer a contained pilot (signing internal
  audit logs, workload identity) where blast radius is zero.

**Tactics:** Breach-cost framing (fault tolerance = lower incident cost); one-page
"what happens when a key is stolen" comparison vs PKI; talk tracks at CISO forums, not
identity conferences; the *stolen credentials* and *expect-detect-contain-recover*
themed clips as cold-outreach material.

**Call to action:** A 2-week proof of concept: duplicity-detectable signing of one
internal artifact chain.

## Persona 2 — "Marcus", the Regulator / Policy Maker / Legal Counsel

**Who:** Writes or enforces eIDAS-like rules, KYC/AML policy, or corporate-governance
law. Wants accountability and recourse, is allergic to "crypto" hype and to systems
that cannot model legal authority.

**Value tags:** LEGOVPOL, ORGID, RECOUPEERS, REALMPRAGM, 1PERSCONTR, CANMODELRW, PROVCONTXT

**Key message:** *"KERI is the only identity layer that can model real-world legal
authority — who signed, under which delegated mandate, with recourse on identifiable
peers — without a blockchain or a foreign trust anchor."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"Is this another blockchain scheme?"* → No token, no ledger, no shared governance
  required (NOSHARSECR). It is key management plus verifiable data structures.
- *"Where is the legal embedding?"* → That is the point: KERI makes no sense *without*
  legal embedding (NOUSEKERI). Show vLEI's ecosystem governance framework as the template.
- *"Privacy vs accountability?"* → SPAC trilemma framing: gradual lifting of
  confidentiality with accountability, not anonymity and not surveillance.

**Tactics:** White papers co-authored with law scholars (Phil Windley's "legal foundation"
talk is the anchor clip); briefings framed around liability and recourse, not cryptography;
map KERI concepts to existing legal instruments (Ricardian contracts, delegation of
authority, corporate officers' signatures).

**Call to action:** Include verifiable organizational identity (vLEI-style) in one
regulatory sandbox or consultation response.

## Persona 3 — "Dev", the Software Engineer / Architect

**Who:** Will actually build the thing. Judges technologies in an afternoon by their
docs, quickstart, and API ergonomics. Skeptical of identity-space hand-waving; respects
solved hard problems.

**Value tags:** HARPROB1ST, NOWSTACK, EASEOFUSE, AGNOSTIC, UNBROCHAIN, GRASPEASY, MATURITY

**Key message:** *"KERI already solved the problem you'd otherwise bolt together from
five libraries — persistent identifiers with rotatable keys, verifiable event logs, and
delegation — and it's cryptography- and vendor-agnostic, like git for identity."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"The learning curve is brutal."* → True today (GRASPEASY is a missing value). Lead
  with KERIA/Signify tutorials and higher-level APIs; hide KERIpy internals.
- *"Docs and tooling?"* → Point to KERIA tutorial, Signify architecture talks, and the
  growing implementations; invite contribution rather than promising perfection.
- *"Why not just DIDs/JWTs?"* → did:webs and the DID-bridge story: KERI interoperates,
  it doesn't compete for your endpoints.

**Tactics:** A 15-minute quickstart that ends with a verifiable credential exchanged
between two agents; "git analogy" explainer (UNBROCHAIN — hash-chained event logs,
versioning, backward compatibility); hackathon bounties; developer-first clips (Kent
Bull, Fergal O'Connor) on dev channels, not marketing channels.

**Call to action:** Run the KERIA tutorial; file one issue or PR.

## Persona 4 — "Elena", the Business Decision Maker / Product Executive

**Who:** Owns a P&L. Cares about onboarding cost, fraud losses, compliance overhead,
and time-to-market. Will not fund infrastructure without a business case.

**Value tags:** BIZEFFCASE, ATSCALE, ECONINFEA, EASEOFUSE, INFRASURF, MARKETPEN

**Key message:** *"Verifiable organizational credentials collapse onboarding and KYC
from weeks to minutes, cut fraud losses structurally, and run at global scale on the
infrastructure you already have."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"Where's the ROI?"* → Endorsement lifts onboarding and research costs (BIZEFFCASE);
  quantify one workflow (supplier onboarding, contract verification) end-to-end.
- *"Network effects — who else is on it?"* → GLEIF and regulated finance first; being
  early in your vertical is the moat, not the risk.
- *"Switching cost?"* → No rip-and-replace: credentials wrap and enrich existing data
  formats (VERCOMTMT), surf existing rails (INFRASURF).

**Tactics:** One-page ROI calculators per vertical (trade finance, healthcare records,
contract credentials); case-study clips (Cole Davis on contract credentials, Alan Davies
on enterprise ACDCs, Jared Jefferey on FHIR/CMS); pilot pricing that matches the
"contained experiment" budget line.

**Call to action:** Fund a single-workflow pilot with a measurable cost baseline.

## Persona 5 — "Priya", the SSI / Digital-Identity Community Skeptic

**Who:** Deep in the DID/VC world. Has heard KERI maximalism before and found the
community abrasive or the claims overreaching. Technically capable of evaluating the
claims — the highest-leverage convert.

**Value tags:** VERNOTVAL, MINIMSUFFI, CANMODELRW, SPACTRILL, GRALIFCONF, NOSHARSECR, OTHUNSAFE

**Key message:** *"Judge the tradespace, not the tribe: KERI is the only design that
gets persistent identifiers without a ledger, and it bridges to the DID world instead
of replacing it."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"KERI people say everything else is broken."* → Reframe OTHUNSAFE as a threat-model
  argument, not an insult: show the specific failure (ephemeral DIDs, shared secrets,
  ledger lock-in) and let them verify.
- *"It's a walled garden."* → did:webs, DID bridge, Trust-Spanning-Protocol work — show
  the interop investments, not the rhetoric.
- *"Too complex for what it adds."* → Minimally sufficient means: everything in KERI
  exists to solve a problem they already acknowledge (key rotation, duplicity).

**Tactics:** Respectful head-to-head tradespace analyses (Samuel Smith's tradespace talk
is the anchor); joint work in DIF/ToIP working groups; invite them to poke holes —
converting one respected skeptic publicly is worth fifty ads.

**Call to action:** Co-author or review one interop spec or comparison document.

## Persona 6 — "Alex", the End User / Privacy Advocate

**Who:** Not a cryptographer. Tired of passwords, phone-number harvesting, profiling
errors they can't correct, and platforms that own their identity. Includes journalists,
NGOs, and everyday citizens.

**Value tags:** 1PERSCONTR, SAFETY, RECOUPEERS, GRALIFCONF, INCLUSIVE, COMMUNEDU, PERPETUAL

**Key message:** *"Your identity should belong to you for life — no usernames, no
passwords, no platform that can delete you, and recourse when someone's data about you
is wrong."*

**Objections & counters:**
- *"Sounds like crypto bros."* → No coins, no speculation, free and open source
  (INCLUSIVE). It is plumbing, like email.
- *"I'll lose my keys."* → Guardianship and delegation are designed in; recovery is a
  first-class feature (FAULTTOL), not an afterthought.
- *"Privacy tools never catch on."* → This one hides inside services they already use;
  they adopt it the way they adopted HTTPS: invisibly.

**Tactics:** Story-first content (the AI-deepfake and stolen-credential themed clips);
plain-language explainers with zero protocol vocabulary (COMMUNEDU); partner with
consumer-facing wallets (Evan Asawaka's mobile-wallet talk shows it's real); "create
personas, consent through transparency" as the tangible feature.

**Call to action:** Install a KERI-backed wallet pilot and verify one credential.

## Cross-persona campaign plan

**Sequencing (beachhead strategy).** Do not chase all six at once. The dependency chain is:

1. **Regulators + CISOs** (Personas 1–2) legitimize — regulated verticals are where
   "verify, not validate" is a compliance requirement, not a nice-to-have.
2. **Business execs** (Persona 4) fund pilots once the compliance story exists.
3. **Developers** (Persona 3) build once there are funded pilots and decent docs.
4. **Community skeptics** (Persona 5) convert once there is evidence, and their
   conversion is the credibility multiplier.
5. **End users** (Persona 6) arrive last and invisibly, inside products.

**Fix the missing values while campaigning.** The graph is honest about the gaps; each
gap has an owner in this plan:

| Missing value | What it takes | Which persona it unblocks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MARKETPEN | Lighthouse deployments + this persona campaign | All |
| GRASPEASY | Analogy-first explainers (git, HTTPS), 90-second clips | Dev, End user |
| MATURITY | Path-to-production work (KERIA/Signify), test coverage, audits | CISO, Business |
| COMMUNEDU | Clip reels, tutorials, plain-language canon | All |

**Measurement.** Per persona: reel view-through rate, call-to-action completions
(pilots started, tutorials completed, specs co-authored), and one named champion per
quarter. Market penetration is a lagging indicator; champions are the leading one.

**The clip reels.** Every persona section above has a matching reel assembled from the
KERICONF26 segments whose value tags intersect the persona's tags — the strongest
30–120 second moments, different speakers, played with the gallery clip player
(`#<videoId>&t=<start>&e=<end>`). Regenerate with `npm run build-personas` after the
clip data updates.
